HOW IT WORKS

  1. 01Drop an EPUB — the file stays on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.
  2. 02Spine reads the book's own table of contents and detects every chapter, in reading order.
  3. 03Preview any chapter, then download all of them as one clean .zip of .txt files.

Files are processed locally in your browser and never leave your device.
