Privacy Policy

Last updated: Month DD, YYYY

The short version

3NDING doesn't have required user accounts, doesn't run analytics or ad trackers, and doesn't sell data. By default, almost everything the site "remembers" about you lives only in your own browser. The one exception is an optional "cloud sync" feature, described below, that you have to deliberately turn on and sign into — it's off for everyone until they choose otherwise.

What's stored, and where

Reading progress, favorites, and reading settings (font, size, theme) are saved using your browser's local storage. This data:

  • Stays on your device — it's never transmitted to a server, unless you turn on cloud sync (see below)
  • Isn't tied to your name, email, or any identity, unless you turn on cloud sync
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data, or using the "Reset to defaults" control in Reading Settings

Optional cloud sync

The "Cloud sync" control in the Browse panel and Reading Settings lets you carry your favorites, reading progress, and reading settings between your own devices, instead of starting over on each one. It is off by default and stays off unless you tap it and sign in with a Google account.

If you turn it on, that data — only that data, nothing else about you or your device — is stored in a Firebase (Google Cloud) database under an entry tied to your Google account's ID, and Firebase's rules are set so only you can read or write that entry. You can turn sync off again at any time from the same control; that stops any further syncing immediately. Turning it off does not automatically delete what was already synced — to have that entry deleted, get in touch through the about & contact page ↗.

Server logs

Like most web servers, the infrastructure hosting 3NDING may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type, and page requested) for a short period, purely to keep the service running and to catch abuse.

Cookies

3NDING itself doesn't set cookies. If you visit the support page ↗ and choose to donate, the payment provider you're redirected to will handle that transaction under its own privacy policy — 3NDING never sees your payment details.

Third parties

Fonts and book files are served directly from 3NDING's own infrastructure. The payment provider on the support page is one third party, used only if you choose to donate. The other is Google/Firebase, used only if you choose to turn on cloud sync — in that case, Google handles the sign-in and the storage of your synced data under its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

3NDING isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, since it doesn't collect personal information at all unless someone deliberately turns on cloud sync.

Changes to this policy

If what 3NDING stores or how it's stored ever changes, this page will be updated and the date above will reflect that.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the address on the about & contact page ↗.
