Privacy Policy

Last updated: Month DD, YYYY

The short version

3NDING doesn't have user accounts, doesn't run analytics or ad trackers, and doesn't sell or share data — because it barely collects any. Almost everything the site "remembers" about you lives only in your own browser.

What's stored, and where

Reading progress, favorites, and reading settings (font, size, theme) are saved using your browser's local storage. This data:

  • Stays on your device — it's never transmitted to a server
  • Isn't tied to your name, email, or any identity
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data, or using the "Reset to defaults" control in Reading Settings
Data Where it lives Purpose
Favorited titles Browser local storage Show your favorites list
Reading progress per book Browser local storage Resume where you left off
Font, size, theme, alignment Browser local storage Remember your reading preferences

Server logs

Like most web servers, the infrastructure hosting 3NDING may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type, and page requested) for a short period, purely to keep the service running and to catch abuse.

Cookies

3NDING itself doesn't set cookies. If you visit the support page ↗ and choose to donate, the payment provider you're redirected to will handle that transaction under its own privacy policy — 3NDING never sees your payment details.

Third parties

Fonts and book files are served directly from 3NDING's own infrastructure. The only third party involved is the payment provider on the support page, and only if you choose to use it.

Children's privacy

3NDING isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, since it doesn't collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If what 3NDING stores or how it's stored ever changes, this page will be updated and the date above will reflect that.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the address on the about & contact page ↗.
