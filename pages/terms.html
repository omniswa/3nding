Terms of Use

Last updated: Month DD, YYYY

1. What 3NDING is

3NDING is a small, independently run archive for reading books in the browser. There's no account system beyond your own device — your reading progress, settings, and favorites are stored locally in your browser and never sent to a server.

2. Using the archive

You're welcome to read, browse, and share links to 3NDING freely. In using it, please don't:

  • Attempt to disrupt, overload, or scrape the site at a scale that affects other readers
  • Use the archive to distribute malware or attempt unauthorized access to its infrastructure
  • Misrepresent your use of the site or impersonate its operator

3. The books themselves

Titles on 3NDING are included in good faith as public-domain works / properly licensed works. If you believe a work has been included in error or without proper rights, please get in touch ↗ and it will be reviewed and removed if warranted.
