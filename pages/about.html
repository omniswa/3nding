About & Contact

3NDING is a small, independently run archive for reading books in the browser — no accounts, no ads, no algorithm deciding what you read next. Just a shelf.

Who's behind it

3NDING is built and kept up by yours truly, a reader who wanted a quieter place to read and discover books.
